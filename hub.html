The 200-bp sample-presence call is positive when samtools depth -aa -Q 30 -q 20 reports nonzero depth at any base in the window; each sample contributes at most one. Presence count is a number of positive samples, and presence fraction is positive samples divided by 166. Neither is read count or expression.

The 166 samples comprise 38 Batch 1 and 128 Batch 2 samples. Existing raw-virus alignment results were reused; no FASTQ remapping was done. Both aggregate presence and representative coverage tracks are based on viral alignments, without uniform host-competition filtering across both batches.
