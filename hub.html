OsHV-1 NC_005881.2 (207,439 bp)

All 110 samples with qualifying viral reads are represented by individual tracks. Ten representative higher-signal samples use true-depth bigWig peaks. The other 100 samples use one-row, 200-bp native positional heatmaps, where color encodes log1p(mean per-base depth) and windows with no qualifying depth are blank. See heatmap methods and color bounds.

The 200-bp sample-presence call is positive when samtools depth -aa -Q 30 -q 20 reports nonzero depth at any base in the window; each sample contributes at most one. Presence count is the number of positive samples, and presence fraction is positive samples divided by 166. Neither is read count or expression.

The 166 samples comprise 38 Batch 1 and 128 Batch 2 samples. Existing raw-virus alignment results were reused; no FASTQ remapping was done. Both aggregate presence and sample coverage tracks are based on viral alignments, without uniform host-competition filtering across both batches. See presence methods and candidate evidence and sequences.
